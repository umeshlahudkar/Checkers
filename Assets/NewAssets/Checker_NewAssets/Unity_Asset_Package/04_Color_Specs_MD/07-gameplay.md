## Player cards
- Card background: #221c2b
- Inactive card border: transparent · Active (current turn) card border: #ff6d5a + soft glow
- Opponent avatar ring: #f3ebdd (their piece color) · Your avatar ring: #ff6d5a
- Name text: #f6f0ea
- Captured-piece pips: filled with the CAPTURED side's piece color (small dots)
- Time (inactive): #a99fb0 · Time (active/your turn): #ff6d5a
